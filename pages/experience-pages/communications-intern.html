Communications Intern

Organization: University of Virginia Facilities Management • Location: Charlottesville, VA • November 2022 – August 2025

As a Communications Intern, I developed engaging content and media for 1,000+ staff members, supporting internal marketing and engagement initiatives. I coordinated event promotion, managed newsletters and social media, and gained hands-on experience in graphic design and strategic communications.

Overview

University of Virginia Facilities Management oversees all physical infrastructure and support services for the university. The communications team works to keep the 1,000+ staff members informed, engaged, and connected through internal communications, newsletters, events, and digital media.

Key Responsibilities

  • Content Development: Created engaging written content, reports, and articles for internal newsletters reaching 1,000+ staff
  • Event Coordination: Promoted campus events, coordinated media coverage, and ensured consistent university branding across promotional materials
  • Social Media Management: Managed social media accounts, created posts, and engaged with followers to build community
  • Graphic Design: Designed digital and print materials using Adobe Creative Suite, maintaining brand consistency
  • Project Leadership: Independently managed communications and social media for 4 months during supervisor's leave, handling all aspects of the role

Major Achievements

  • Developed a consistent content calendar that increased staff engagement by creating timely, relevant updates
  • Designed and launched refreshed newsletter templates improving readability and visual appeal
  • Created promotional campaigns for major university events with coordinated graphics, copy, and social media
  • Managed social media independently, maintaining posting schedule and community engagement during extended absence
  • Produced high-quality digital and print assets using Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign

Skills Developed

  • Communications: Professional writing, storytelling, audience segmentation, message clarity
  • Design: Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign), visual hierarchy, brand consistency
  • Digital Marketing: Social media strategy, content calendar management, engagement metrics
  • Project Management: Multi-task juggling, deadline management, independent initiative
  • Stakeholder Engagement: Understanding diverse audiences, creating inclusive communications
