Curse and Cure

Role: Developer • Tools: Unity, C# • April 2025 – May 2025

A top-down RPG game developed with a team. I focused on implementing core gameplay mechanics including grid-based movement, interactive NPC dialogue systems, collision detection, and custom sprite/tilemap integration to create an immersive player experience.

Back to projects

Overview

Curse and Cure is a top-down RPG where players navigate a fantasy world, interact with NPCs, and engage with puzzle and combat mechanics. The game emphasizes player agency and immersive storytelling through dialogue and environmental design.

My Role & Contributions

As a core developer on this project, I was responsible for implementing key gameplay systems:

  • Grid-based Movement System: Designed and implemented player movement constrained to a grid, enabling precise positioning and tile-based level design
  • NPC Dialogue System: Built an interactive dialogue system allowing players to engage with NPCs through conversation trees
  • Collision Detection: Implemented collision systems to prevent player clipping and enable interactive objects
  • Visual Design: Integrated custom sprites and tilemaps to enhance the visual aesthetic and world-building

Process

1

Concept & Planning

Collaborated with team to define core gameplay mechanics, narrative structure, and visual direction for the RPG.

2

Implementation

Developed core systems in Unity, starting with player movement mechanics and expanding to dialogue and collision handling.

3

Integration

Integrated custom sprites, tilemaps, and audio assets into the game engine.

4

Testing & Iteration

Playtested gameplay mechanics, iterated on feel and responsiveness, fixed bugs and edge cases.

5

Polish

Refined visual presentation, dialogue flow, and overall user experience based on playtester feedback.

Technical Details

Engine: Unity • Language: C# • Key Systems: Movement Controller, Dialogue Manager, Collision System, Sprite Renderer

Key Learnings

This project taught me the importance of modular, scalable code architecture in game development. Building reusable systems for dialogue and movement made it easy to expand gameplay later. I also learned how sprite and animation timing significantly impacts player perception of game responsiveness.
