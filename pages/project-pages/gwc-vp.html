About Girls Who Code

Girls Who Code is a national nonprofit dedicated to closing the gender gap in computing. College Loops are student-led chapters that bring the GWC mission to college campuses, creating community and opportunities for women in tech through events, workshops, and professional development.

My Role & Responsibilities

  • Executive Leadership: Co-lead executive team with president to set strategic direction and oversee club operations
  • Event Coordination: Plan, promote, and execute major events including annual hackathon and career fair attracting 100+ students
  • Curriculum Development: Design engaging workshops and industry events that build technical and professional skills
  • Member Engagement: Create inclusive community and foster connections among club members
  • Industry Partnerships: Build relationships with tech companies and professionals to bring speakers and mentors to campus
  • Organizational Management: Oversee budget, communications, and day-to-day club operations

Key Initiatives & Impact

  • Annual Hackathon: Organized inclusive coding event bringing together hundreds of students and industry professionals
  • Career Fair: Coordinated recruitment event connecting members with tech companies and career opportunities
  • Technical Workshops: Developed and facilitated hands-on workshops in various tech domains
  • Mentorship Program: Established connections between students and professionals in tech
  • Community Building: Created welcoming environment where women in tech feel supported and inspired

Core Values & Commitment

  • Inclusivity: Create space where all women feel welcome, regardless of background or experience level
  • Empowerment: Build confidence and equip members with skills to pursue tech careers
  • Community: Foster connections and friendships that extend beyond the organization
  • Excellence: Develop high-quality programs that leave lasting impact on members
  • Advocacy: Champion women in tech and work to increase diversity in the tech industry
