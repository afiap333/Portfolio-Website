Hoos Studying

Role: Project Lead & UX Designer • Tools: Figma, User Research • January – May 2024

A mobile app prototype designed to optimize student study environments at UVA. I led a team of 5 through the complete UX design lifecycle, from user research and persona development to interactive prototyping in Figma.

Back to projects

Overview

Hoos Studying addresses a real problem at University of Virginia: students struggle to find suitable study spaces. The app helps students discover, reserve, and share feedback about study locations across campus, considering factors like noise level, amenities, capacity, and atmosphere.

My Role & Contributions

As Project Lead, I owned the entire UX design process and team coordination:

  • User Research: Led interviews and surveys with 30+ UVA students to understand study habits, pain points, and preferences
  • Analysis & Strategy: Synthesized research findings into actionable personas, user journeys, and design requirements
  • Design Direction: Established design system and visual language for the app
  • Prototyping: Created high-fidelity interactive prototypes in Figma for user testing
  • Iteration: Conducted multiple rounds of user feedback and refined designs based on learnings
  • Team Leadership: Managed a team of 5, coordinated workstreams, and ensured design cohesion

Process

1

Research

Conducted user interviews, surveys, and observational studies. Identified key user segments and critical pain points.

2

Analysis

Synthesized data into user personas, empathy maps, and user journeys that informed all subsequent design decisions.

3

Ideation

Brainstormed solutions with the team, sketched wireframes, and defined core app features and user flows.

4

Design

Created comprehensive Figma prototypes including onboarding, study space discovery, booking, and social features.

5

Validation

Conducted moderated user testing sessions, gathered feedback, and iterated on designs for maximum usability.

Key Design Insights

  • Context Matters: Students prioritized different features depending on study type (individual focus vs. group collaboration)
  • Real-time Info: Occupancy and noise levels in real-time were critical decision factors
  • Social Features: Users wanted to share study spots with friends and read peer reviews
  • Accessibility: Information about study space accessibility (parking, wheelchair access) was important

Design System & Features

Core Features: Study Space Discovery Map • Detailed Space Profiles • Booking/Reservation • Real-time Occupancy Status • User Reviews & Photos • Social Sharing • Personalized Recommendations

Design Elements: Card-based layouts for space browsing • Interactive map integration • Star rating system • Tag-based filtering • Push notifications • Dark mode support

Impact & Learnings

Leading this project taught me the power of user-centered design. By investing time in understanding our users deeply, we created solutions that genuinely solved their problems. Key lessons: empathy-driven research is invaluable, iteration based on real user feedback beats assumptions, and clear communication across a team is essential for design cohesion.
