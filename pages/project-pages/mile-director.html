About MILE

The Muslim Institute for Leadership and Empowerment (MILE) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to developing young Muslim leaders through mentorship, professional development, and community engagement. The program creates pathways for students to grow their leadership skills and make meaningful impact in their communities.

My Role & Responsibilities

  • Recruitment Strategy: Developed comprehensive strategies to identify and attract qualified students to the program
  • Content Creation: Created compelling written, visual, and video content showcasing program value and student success stories
  • Social Media Management: Built and executed social media strategy using short-form video and visual storytelling
  • Analytics & Data: Used analytics to understand audience, measure campaign effectiveness, and optimize performance
  • Brand Development: Led graphic design processes to create cohesive, recognizable brand identity
  • Campaign Execution: Planned and executed recruitment campaigns across multiple channels
  • Team Coordination: Collaborated with team to align messaging and ensure consistent brand representation

Key Initiatives & Outcomes

  • Short-Form Video Campaigns: Created engaging TikTok and Instagram Reels showcasing student testimonials and program highlights
  • Brand Redesign: Developed modern, cohesive visual identity that appeals to student demographic
  • Social Media Growth: Built engaged following through consistent, high-quality content
  • Recruitment Campaigns: Executed multi-channel campaigns that increased program applications and enrollment
  • Visual Analytics: Used data-driven insights to optimize content and improve campaign ROI
  • Community Partnerships: Built relationships with student organizations and community groups to amplify reach
