Overview

University of Virginia Facilities Management oversees all physical infrastructure and support services for the university, with a team of 1,000+ staff members across various departments. The communications team plays a crucial role in keeping staff informed, engaged, and connected through strategic internal communications, newsletters, events, and digital media.

My Role & Contributions

  • Content Development: Created engaging written content, reports, and articles for internal newsletters reaching 1,000+ staff
  • Event Coordination: Promoted campus events, coordinated media coverage, and ensured consistent university branding across promotional materials
  • Social Media Management: Managed social media accounts, created posts, engaged with followers, and built community
  • Graphic Design: Designed digital and print materials using Adobe Creative Suite, maintaining brand consistency
  • Independent Leadership: Managed all communications and social media for 4 months during supervisor's leave—handling strategy, execution, and staff coordination

Key Projects & Achievements

  • Developed a consistent content calendar that increased staff engagement through timely, relevant updates
  • Designed and launched refreshed newsletter templates improving readability and visual appeal
  • Created promotional campaigns for major university events with coordinated graphics, copy, and social media strategy
  • Managed social media independently for 4 months, maintaining posting schedule and community engagement
  • Produced high-quality digital and print assets using Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign
